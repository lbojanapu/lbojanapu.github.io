Vynqel Keys

Help keep independent ideas moving.

Vynqel Keys is built with a simple belief: a keyboard should feel thoughtful, useful and respectful of the person using it.

It is an independent project, developed with care and a focus on an offline-first typing experience. If you find Vynqel Keys useful and would like to support its continued development, you are welcome to contribute.

Visit the developer on GitHub

What your support helps with

  • Ongoing maintenance, testing and compatibility improvements.
  • Improving the typing experience while keeping the product lightweight.
  • Research and development of useful offline capabilities.
  • Time and resources needed to keep an independent project moving.

Entirely voluntary. No pressure.

Supporting Vynqel Keys is completely optional. The app can be used without making a contribution. Please contribute only if you want to and can comfortably afford to do so.

A contribution is a gesture of support, not a purchase. It does not unlock features, provide special access, guarantee future functionality, or create any subscription or service entitlement.

Thank you

Whether you contribute, share feedback, report a problem, or simply use the keyboard, your support means a lot. Independent projects grow because people choose to give them a chance.

Thank you for being part of the Vynqel journey.
