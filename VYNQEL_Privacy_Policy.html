Vynqel Keys — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · App: Vynqel Keys · Android package: com.bojanapu.vynqel

Developer: Lakshmi Narayana Bojanapu
Brand / organisation: Bojanapu PVT Ltd

Vynqel Keys is an offline-first Android keyboard. This policy explains how the app processes information when you use it.

1. Information the keyboard processes

When you type with Vynqel Keys, the keyboard processes the text around the cursor so it can insert characters and display word suggestions. The app's prediction feature also uses the current word and a limited amount of preceding-word context to calculate suggestions.

If personal learning is active for an input field, Vynqel Keys stores learned words and short word-transition counts in an app-private database on your device. This information is used to improve suggestions on that device. Typing-feedback preferences are also stored locally.

The app is designed to process this information on your device. The reviewed Android app source does not declare the Android INTERNET permission and does not include an account or cloud-sync feature.

2. Information we collect or share

In the reviewed app implementation, Vynqel Keys does not send typed text, learned vocabulary, prediction history, or keyboard preferences to the developer's servers. The app does not provide an account-registration feature, advertising feature, or analytics feature.

Android and the device manufacturer provide the operating system, keyboard framework, and system services. Their own processing is governed by their respective privacy terms and device settings.

3. Sensitive fields and personal learning

Vynqel Keys is designed to suppress personalized learning and predictions in password-related fields and fields that request no personalized learning. The behaviour can depend on the input field's configuration supplied by the app in which you are typing.

Do not use any third-party keyboard to enter information unless you are comfortable with the keyboard's operation. You can switch to another keyboard at any time through Android's input-method picker or system settings.

4. Local storage, retention and deletion

Learned words, word-transition counts, and keyboard feedback preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are retained until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android's storage and device-management behaviour.

The current app does not provide a separate in-app control to erase only learned vocabulary. To remove the app's locally stored data, use Android Settings → Apps → Vynqel Keys → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data resets local preferences and learning.

5. External links

The Settings screen includes an optional link to the developer's GitHub profile for support and voluntary contributions. If you choose to open that link, your browser will connect to GitHub. GitHub may process information under its own privacy policy. Vynqel Keys does not control that external service.

Developer GitHub profile: https://github.com/lbojanapu.

6. Children's privacy

Vynqel Keys is a general-purpose keyboard and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children. The app does not ask users to create accounts or submit personal information to the developer.

7. Security

Local learning data is stored in the app's private Android storage. Device security, screen locks, operating-system updates, and physical access controls remain important. No method of storage or software can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its store listing where appropriate.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer, Lakshmi Narayana Bojanapu, through the Vynqel developer GitHub profile. Bojanapu PVT Ltd is the brand / organisation associated with Vynqel Keys. Please do not include passwords or sensitive text in support requests.
